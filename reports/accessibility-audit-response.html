Where the fixes live

Many findings sit in our shared component library, @infonomic/uikit, rather than the app itself — the search field, select, checkbox, copy button, pagination and tabs. Those were fixed centrally and released as 6.7.7, so they resolve everywhere the component is used, not just on the page where each was reported.

How we verified

Type-checking and linting on every change; the app driven in-browser (light & dark themes, keyboard operation, rendered-DOM inspection). The tab keyboard behaviour and sign-in page were confirmed hands-on. Remaining items await a signed-in screen-reader pass over the authenticated dashboard.

“Pages per view” has role="combobox"
Assessed as a false positive. The control renders role="combobox" with the full supporting ARIA (aria-haspopup, aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-labelledby) — the valid ARIA 1.2 select-only combobox pattern. Removing the role would break it. We’d welcome the auditor’s confirmation.
 Learners4.1.2 AMinor

One question for the auditor — finding 24

Finding 24 is the only point where we’d propose not making the recommended change. The role="combobox" reported here is, in our reading, the current WAI-ARIA select-only combobox pattern (a button that expands a listbox), correctly supported by aria-expanded / aria-controls. The “remove the role” guidance matches the older ARIA 1.1 model where combobox required a descendant text input. If you agree, we’ll mark it closed; if you’re seeing a specific AT failure, we’ll dig in further.
